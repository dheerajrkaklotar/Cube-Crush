1. Overview

Welcome to Cube Crush! ("the Game", "we", "our", or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you play Cube Crush! on Android (APK), Progressive Web App (PWA), or Web browsers.

2. Personal Information We Do Not Collect

We do not require you to register an account or log in. The Game does not request or collect personal data such as your name, phone number, physical address, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or precise GPS location.

3. Local Gameplay Storage (On-Device Data)

To save your game progress offline and online, Cube Crush! stores gameplay state locally on your device using browser/WebView LocalStorage:

  • High Scores and Classic Mode difficulty preferences (Easy, Medium, Hard)
  • Daily Streak count and 24-hour resetting Daily Missions progress
  • Remaining Rotate and Mega Bomb hint balances
  • Audio settings (Mute state, Music Volume, and Music Speed)

This data remains on your device and can be reset anytime by clearing your app storage or browser cache.

  • 320×50 Bottom Banner Ads (ca-app-pub-8252748238984276/8476685886)
  • Full-Screen Interstitial Ads between game rounds
  • Optional Full-Screen Rewarded Video Ads (ca-app-pub-8252748238984276/1911277534) when you choose to watch a video ad to earn +1 Rotate or +1 Mega Bomb hint

Google and its partners may use device advertising identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID), cookies, coarse IP location, and ad interaction metrics to provide and measure advertisements in accordance with the Google Privacy Policy. You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your Android device Settings.

5. Children's Privacy

Cube Crush! is a family-friendly block puzzle arcade game. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
